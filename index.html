eBGP Network with ACL Filtering

A Cisco Packet Tracer lab with three 2911 routers in separate autonomous systems: an ISP (AS 300), HQ_Edge (AS 200) and a Branch (AS 100). They peer over eBGP and advertise their LAN networks, and an extended ACL on HQ_Edge blocks one Branch PC from reaching an HQ host while permitting all other traffic.
